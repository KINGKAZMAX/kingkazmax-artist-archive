Cosmic Embodiment

2024–2025 · Completed immersive installation prototype · Lead creator

Role and approach

I developed lunar-exploration scenarios and multimodal interaction around a body–environment–tool framework. The prototype combines UE5 and Blender environments, Pico 4 tracking, Leap Motion gesture input and TouchDesigner screen coordination.

The project supported my 2025 master's thesis and first-authored research.

AI-assisted visual concept study

AETHERA

Speculative lunar habitats · Visual direction and concept development

This concept study explores lunar habitats through speculative architecture and atmospheric visual storytelling. AI-assisted imagery supports studies of material contrast, light, scale and spatial narrative.

I develop visual directions and atmospheric studies, then connect spatial narratives with 3D and interactive prototypes. Future production would follow a project-specific creative and technical brief.
