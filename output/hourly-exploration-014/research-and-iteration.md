# Elin / Silent-film intertitle restorer / R01 candidate

## Research
Source: BFI, Kieron Webb, 20 August 2012, https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/restoring-hitchcock-2-reconstructing-intertitles (checked 2026-10-21).
The BFI account explains that reconstructed intertitles require suitable duration and graphic quality; a completely frozen title can interrupt film rhythm. Its described workflow restores movement and grain through film printing and rescanning. These are source facts, not a claim about all silent-film practices.

## Original interpretation
Elin restores dialogue cards in a fictional travelling archive. She distrusts perfectly polished lettering that erases the rhythm of a damaged reel. Rectangular ivory lapels and a blank chest label echo title-card geometry; aubergine and moss distinguish her from earlier projectionist Mira and rehearsal character Leto. Angular face, low ponytail and impatient asymmetric brows define the identity. This is original fiction, not a depiction of the BFI archivist or a film character. Small ivory studs appeared in generation and are retained consistently.
