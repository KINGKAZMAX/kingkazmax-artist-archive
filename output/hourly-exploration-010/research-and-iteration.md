## Interpretation and character
Sira is an original fictional dawn gate watcher, not Inanna/Ishtar and not a historical reconstruction. A small eight-point ochre star badge connects the design to the researched astral symbol; no invented ritual or historical uniform is asserted. Indigo, terracotta and aubergine are an original palette within the deployed series. Broad cheekbones, a short chin, compact side knot and a defiant asymmetric grin distinguish her from previous rounds. She challenges careless travellers and remembers every promise made at her gate.
