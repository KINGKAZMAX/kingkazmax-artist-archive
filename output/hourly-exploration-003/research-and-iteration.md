## Design proposal
Separate identity traits from camera/rendering variants: the same character remains one identity in frontal and low three-quarter views. This is our design decision, not a rule imposed by the token standard. Define identity using role, hair silhouette/color, eyes, expression, clothing and one accent; avoid arbitrary accessory accumulation and false rarity claims. No minting, wallet transaction or marketplace listing.

Tavi / 塔維 is an original signal repairer who restores messages that arrive out of order. Short sage-green blunt uneven fringe, broad cheekbones, flat blue-grey eyes, uneven knitted brows and a stubborn teeth-baring grin. Ivory work jacket, muted teal collar, navy strap, one ochre square clip. New silhouette and expression distinguish the character from earlier Senu and Rill.

## Style and production
Live characters/data.js matched local source at preflight. Both production style-master PNGs were inspected and passed directly to imagegen. Preserve warm peach skin, decisive tapered black contours, sparse internal lines, hard shadows, flat irises and pure black pupils without eye catchlights. Senu R01 remains rejected and is not a reference. Exact prompts saved separately. Raster art remains raster; Figma labels, research and layout are editable. Website UI and existing collection are unchanged.
