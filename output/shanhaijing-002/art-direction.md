User direction, 2026-10-23: 全部都要俊男靓女，少年少女，可以不同年龄；要有妖怪特征结合，参考平安京的设定。

## Scope and design rules

All distinctive beasts, deities, unusual peoples and unusual plants across eighteen chapters. Individual mountains, rivers and minerals are excluded. The inventory is still being audited; the current Southern Mountains studies are not the complete book.

All identities use attractive youthful human faces, varying adolescent/young-adult age impression, face shape, hairstyle and temperament. No rough middle-aged or elderly faces. Creature anatomy must be organic and readable: ears, horns, feathers, scales, tails or eyes, selected from the relevant text. Humanized faces and clothing are original inventions. Clothing icons alone are insufficient.

Onmyoji Arena is a conceptual reference for human/yokai integration. The official site identifies its Onmyoji lineage and varied visual treatments. It is not the rendering master and does not authorize a change to glossy game illustration.
Official reference: https://www.onmyojiarena.tw/
Source mythology: https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=山海經/南山經&oldid=2650929

Rendering masters remain assets/characters/natural-anime-02/portrait-01.png and portrait-02.png, both directly attached to generation. Near-black variable-weight outlines, sparse internal lines, flat warm peach skin, hard-edged shadows, simple iris and solid black pupils without highlights. Transparent outside silhouettes, opaque character surfaces, no aura or vignette.
