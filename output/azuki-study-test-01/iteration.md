# Azuki study / test 01

Scope: first-image test before a 30-image production run. Two reference originals downloaded; this is not a complete 10,000-token archive. No website UI or production gallery replacement.

Sources:

Observed in these samples: near-profile bust, pale neutral background, tapered black outlines, flat opaque colors, sparse hard-edged shadows, graphic hair locks, readable streetwear silhouette. #7738 uses a long diagonal broom and scarf; #6195 pairs a red hood with a takeaway cup. These observations describe two samples, not every token. Clothing, headgear and offhand are explicit trait categories on the collection page; occupations are creative interpretations rather than verified collection metadata.

Adaptation: approved KINGKAZMAX black-haired goggle character supplies eyebrows, amber eyes, surprised open mouth and simplified facial language. Red work jacket, dark hoodie, courier harness and diagonal tool support a courier/mechanic identity. Camera above subject, enlarged head, receding torso, chin raised and head turned sideways. Both eyes open.

Review: above-camera geometry and expression are clear. Fisheye edge curvature is restrained; a subsequent iteration can exaggerate it. Await user direction before remaining 29. Raster assets remain raster fills in Figma; page names, arrangement and analysis text are editable, not layered character line art.
