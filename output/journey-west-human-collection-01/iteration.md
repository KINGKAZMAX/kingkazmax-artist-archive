# Journey to the West / first thirty human youths / R02

User requested classic Journey to the West characters with innovation informed by Black Myth and related animation/games, permitting an additional-character proposal beyond thirty. During initial generation the user explicitly changed ALL portraits to attractive youthful human men/women, allowing gender reversals and character contrast. The authoritative delivery follows this correction. Fifteen earlier animal-faced/aged drafts are retained as superseded history in output/journey-west-collection-01/superseded/ and must not appear on the final board or in the final download ZIP.

Regenerated Sun Wukong as a handsome youthful human master, followed by twenty-nine distinct new human portraits. Common layout: left-facing near-profile eye-level head/upper-chest crop, true alpha PNG, signature prop and clean cel-color graphic rendering consistent with the live collection. Humans retain normal noses, lips, ears and clean jawlines. Animal identity appears only through garment motifs, ornamental headpieces and props.

Gender reimaginings include a playful female Zhu Bajie, composed female Sha Wujing, youthful feminine Rulai/Taishang/Zhenyuan and a female Nezha. Tang Sanzang, Guanyin, Rulai, Taishang and Bodhi intentionally have closed eyes. Remaining portraits use open expressive eyes and varied brows/mouths.

Research sources and adaptation decisions: research.md. Exact common prompt, per-character brief and master prompt: prompts.md. Proposed next eighteen are in expansion-candidates.md, pending approval. This delivery does not silently exceed thirty characters. No website deployment was requested; fifty published website dossiers remain unchanged.

Figma assets are raster PNG fills with editable card labels and board layout, not layered vector character illustration. Source commit, asset IDs and sync confirmation belong in the project manifest.

## Verification

All thirty final files decoded as 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNGs; alpha extrema 0–255; 24.95–40.21% fully transparent pixels. Reviewed three ten-character boards: all thirty have youthful human facial anatomy, consistent left-facing profile framing, distinct costume/prop cues, no aged beards or animal muzzles. Five intentional closed-eye characters. Design briefs specify 16 male and 14 female interpretations. White review-board background is a preview only; source PNGs retain alpha.
