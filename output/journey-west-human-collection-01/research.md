## Original design decisions

Keep square, eye-level left-facing side-profile upper-chest crop and a readable personal prop. Retain tapered black outline, flat colored eye, graphic eyebrow/mouth, warm skin and limited hard cel-shadow shapes. Texture-heavy game rendering is simplified into broad cloth/armor color planes. Latest user correction: ALL thirty have beautiful youthful HUMAN faces. Species cues appear only through symbolic hair accessories, garment colors and signature props. No animal facial anatomy, aged faces or beards.

Use specific expressions: closed-eyed reflective pilgrims/teachers; stern celestial figures; proud, anxious, mischievous or aggressive demons. Some closed-eye portraits are deliberate character decisions, expressly authorized by the user. Do not claim every selected literary character appears in Black Myth or that these are canonical in-game designs.
