# Journey to the West / Sun Wukong test / R01

User brief: a new 30-character Chinese Journey to the West mythology collection. Test Sun Wukong first. Azuki composition informs side-profile bust framing, readable clothing and a diagonal signature prop. Expression and rendering continue the currently published KINGKAZMAX collection. Some later characters may have closed eyes, including Tang Sanzang. Sun Wukong uses one visible open eye in near-full side profile.

Direction: monkey warrior identity rather than a generic human costume; golden fur, peach bare facial mask, rounded ear, compact swept hair, gold tightening circlet, red scarf, ochre tunic, restrained shoulder armor and Ruyi Jingu Bang behind the shoulder. Lively challenging eyebrow and open grin. Transparent background inherited from the accepted production requirements.

The new side-profile instruction supersedes the preceding overhead camera for this collection. Reference 1 and 2 control live linework, flat eye and cel-color vocabulary, while Azuki #7738 controls pose, crop and diagonal prop. A sample-based composition analysis does not claim that every Azuki token has the same layout.

Scope: one test only, thirty-character expansion awaiting review of this direction. No homepage or production dossier change requested at this stage.
