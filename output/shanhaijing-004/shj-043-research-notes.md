## Character interpretation

Tong / 彤, a 19-year-old female pine-seed path scout, sharp and impatient. Beautiful youthful human face, angular charcoal haircut, olive eyes, uneven brows and terse half-open mouth. Black feathers physically grow on the arms/shoulders; a long black pheasant tail emerges behind the waist. Two lower legs transform into exposed vermilion-scaled bird shanks and red clawed feet; they are actual anatomy, not red boots.
Source traits: pheasant-like bird, black body, red feet. Human face, age, gender, scout role, precise feather distribution, cream/black clothing and red cloth sash are original design choices.

[Official Onmyoji Arena homepage](https://www.onmyojiarena.tw/) was checked online for character-lineage and shikigami context.
