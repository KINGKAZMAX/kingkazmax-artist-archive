## Original interpretation
Nao is a human wind observer in a fictional coastal town, not Fujin and not a historical religious costume reconstruction. A soft sand-colored fabric loop behind his neck abstracts the bag silhouette while keeping the portrait uncluttered. Sage and charcoal clothing, a broad forehead, narrow cheeks and a squared short chin distinguish him. His defiant expression comes from arguing with captains who ignore changing winds. The role and palette are invented; no claim of ritual authority or mythology lineage is made.
