# Tiao — Fuyu mallow-like variant / 條（符禺山葵狀）

Source: [西山經](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=山海經/西山經&oldid=2650930), P0048 / MT-P0048-02; verified 2026-10-23T12:28:24.191987+00:00.

> 其草多條，其狀如葵，而赤華黃實，如嬰兒舌，食之使人不惑。

## Source facts

- The 符禺山 variant of 條 is mallow-like (葵).
- Red flowers and yellow fruits, the fruit compared with an infant's tongue.
- Distinct from 石脆山 same-name 條: chive-like, white flowers, black fruit. The project intentionally retains both variants.
- The text attributes freedom from confusion; literary material, not a medical claim.

## Original character design

- A 21-year-old female route adjudicator, plum hair/wrap coat and facial identity.
- Rounded lobed leaf ears, green forearms, paired body-grown red flowers and two yellow elongated botanical pods.
- Exact botanical species, leaf/flower structure, human features and job are design choices; pods are not literal human tongues.
