## Research and sources
BFI's Cinema Paradiso synopsis describes a filmmaker recalling his Sicilian childhood and friendship with a projectionist. This inspires intergenerational craft and the cinema booth as a place of work, rather than copying an actor or costume.

In BFI's 6 June 2023 interview, projectionist Mike Sanderson describes learning from colleagues and trial and error. He compares analogue projection with live performance and recalls adjusting projector speed during a screening. This supports a character occupied with practical timing and concentration.
https://www.bfi.org.uk/interviews/meet-projectionists-mike-sanderson

## Original design interpretation
Mira repairs a small travelling cinema's projectors. She is impatient with interruptions but quietly teaches apprentices how to listen for irregular mechanisms. Her short copper crop, navy clip, teal work collar, ivory shirt and rust apron strap are invented design choices, not a documented historical uniform. Round cheeks, a small chin and a blunt fringe distinguish her from earlier characters. An uneven brow and crooked tense mouth convey annoyed concentration.
