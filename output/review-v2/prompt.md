# KINGKAZMAX / Frontal direction study V2

Status: one approval sample, not a bulk collection replacement.

## Fixed direction

Front-facing head and neck; bold black contours; solid black oval eyes; broad colour fields; one hard-edged shadow tone per material; expression between surprise and uncertainty; genuine transparent PNG alpha.

## Reference roles

1. Aokizy, K (2025), [Arario Gallery source](https://www.arariogallery.com/ko/artworks/19848-aokizy-k-2025/): simplified eyes, sparse facial lines and broad flat planes. Third-party reference is not a KINGKAZMAX work. Its local research copy is excluded from Git.
2. `output/review-v1/kingkazmax-character-v1.png`: project continuity, palette, cutout silhouette. Change pose to frontal.

## Base prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create exactly ONE new original KINGKAZMAX head-and-neck anime character portrait, a first approval sample for a consistent collection. Use reference 1 (Aokizy K 2025) as drawing-language reference only: bold black contours, extremely simplified solid black oval eyes, broad opaque flat color fields, sparse facial marks and hard-edged shadow shapes. Use reference 2 (our approved silver-haired character) as continuity reference for palette, head-and-neck cutout and original character identity, but CHANGE its pose to strict frontal view. FRONT FACING, level head, no tilt, no three-quarter turn, both ears equally visible, gaze directly at viewer. Adult androgynous character with short ash-silver hair organized into 5-7 broad chunky locks, warm muted peach skin, dark navy simple standing collar. Expression frozen BETWEEN SURPRISE AND CONFUSION: slightly raised asymmetric brows, large oval black eyes with no highlights or detailed irises, tiny parted mouth, small angular nose indicated by 1-2 strokes; uncertain, restrained, not angry, not smiling, not shouting. Graphic 2D vector-like finish. Thick tapered outer black outline, minimal inner detail. Strict flat opaque fills and one discrete hard shadow tone for skin, hair and collar, no gradients, no airbrush, no light bloom, no painterly canvas texture, no skin pores, no 3D rendering, no glossy effects. Use 6-8 colors total. Whole head, hair, neck and collar fully inside square canvas, 8% clear padding on every side; even centered scale. TRANSPARENT PNG with actual alpha background, absolutely no white backdrop, checkerboard, scene, frame, sticker border, drop shadow, words, signature, logo or watermark. Recombine the visual principles into a new face; do not trace or reproduce the reference painting. Deliver one portrait only.

## Refinement pass

Preserve identity and strict frontal pose. Replace gradients and soft blush with flat unmodulated fills; one crisp polygonal shadow per material. Simplify interior hair strokes, strengthen outer contours, retain a tiny parted mouth and questioning brows. Preserve true transparent alpha and complete head/neck padding.
