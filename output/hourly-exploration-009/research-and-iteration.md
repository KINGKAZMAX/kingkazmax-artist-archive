## Verified sources

## New subtheme and interpretation
Earlier NFT rounds explored trait design and transparent silhouettes. This round distinguishes persistent character identity from presentation angle. Vale has one identity; front and three-quarter are two views, not two new identities. This is an archive design decision, not a claim that ERC-721 mandates it. No token is minted and no financial claim is made.

## Original character
Vale checks seals in a fictional civic archive. He is skeptical, impatient with vague labels, and quietly proud of accurate records. Ash-blond cropped hair, a single broad forelock, narrow rectangular face, thick brows, olive eyes, ochre collar, navy shirt and a burgundy fabric tab form his visual identity. These choices are invented, not derived from an existing NFT character. His brow and mouth retain tension rather than a neutral polished expression.
