## Original adaptation
Qing / 青: an adult jade-route scout who challenges strangers at the mountain boundary. Rust hair with cream forelock, olive eyes, tense asymmetric brows and mouth, forest-green wrap collar and square jade clasp. Nine-tail motif translated into a shoulder mantle. Face and garments are inventions, not an archaeological reconstruction. Warm peach skin, near-black tapered contour, sparse lines, no eye catchlights, hard cel shadows, transparent cutout.
