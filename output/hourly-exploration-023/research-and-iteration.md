# Tor / Personal inventory / R01 candidate
Source checked 2026-10-21: https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-6551
The official proposal describes accounts bound to NFTs, enabling them to hold assets and interact with applications. Its motivation includes role-playing characters accumulating assets and abilities. It is marked Review on the retrieved page.
Original visual interpretation: distinguish enduring identity from carried possessions. Tor is a traveling keepsake custodian who remembers each object and its owner; one ochre chest pouch represents a small personal inventory. This is character design research only, no token deployment or implemented inventory functionality. Broad jaw, short sandy crop, amber eyes, incredulous brow and crooked open mouth; petrol jacket, brick shirt and pouch on anatomical right chest.
